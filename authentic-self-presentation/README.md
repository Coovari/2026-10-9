# Finding Your Authentic Self or Not

A simple English classroom presentation designed for approximately four minutes.

## Downloads

- [PowerPoint slides](Finding_Your_Authentic_Self.pptx)
- [English speaker script with Chinese prompts](Speaker_Script_EN_ZH.docx)
- [Plain-text speaker script](Speaker_Script_EN_ZH.txt)

For PowerPoint and Word files, open the file and click **Download raw file** (the download arrow) on GitHub.

The deck contains six speaking slides and one reference slide. Speaker notes are included. The script is about 326 English words; delivery time depends on speaking pace and pauses.

The chart is redrawn from selected rows of Table 7 in Damian et al. (2019), *Sixteen going on sixty-six: A longitudinal study of personality stability and change across 50 years*. https://doi.org/10.1037/pspp0000210
